# About Hotel Forest Inn Limited (HFIN)

Historical daily share trading dataset for **Hotel Forest Inn Limited (HFIN)** on the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE).

- **Symbol:** `HFIN`
- **Exchange:** Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE)
- **Sector:** Hotels and Tourism
- **Attributes:** Business date, Close price, High price, Low price, Traded quantity, Traded turnover value, Trade transactions count.
- **Tags:** `stock-data`, `hfin`, `hotel-forest-inn`, `financial-dataset`, `nepse`
